Our story

From one room
to Sukhumvit.

Edmund Thorne arrived in Bangkok in 1991. He'd trained at the London College of Fashion and apprenticed under a Hong Kong master tailor. He found a city with real taste and ambition, and no truly world-class bespoke house.

In 1994 he opened a single-room atelier in Silom with one assistant and a bolt of fine English wool. Within five years, on word of mouth alone, the atelier had outgrown three premises and was cutting for clients from Singapore to Frankfurt.

Today twelve craftspeople work from our three-floor studio on Sukhumvit Road. We buy from more than forty mills and keep over 1,500 bolts in the cloth library.

Choosing cloth

How to choose the
right cloth.

Climate

Dress for Bangkok heat

Linen, tropical-weight wool and fine cotton breathe. Save cashmere and tweed for travel and air-conditioned rooms.

Occasion

Match the cloth to the day

A Super 120s or 150s wool carries authority in the boardroom. Silk blends and fine cashmere suit weddings and evenings.

Wear

Balance comfort and wear

Tighter-twisted yarns resist creasing and last longer. Looser twists feel softer. Your tailor will help you find the balance.
